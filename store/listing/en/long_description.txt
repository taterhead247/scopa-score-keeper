Track your Scopa games without the scrap paper.

Played by Italian families for generations, Scopa is a card game that
rewards memory, strategy — and accurate score-keeping. Scopa Score
brings the scoring out of the margins of napkins and onto your phone,
where it belongs.

What you get:

🎯 Effortless scoring
Pick winners for Cards, Coins, Settebello, and Primiera with a single
tap. Track scopas as they happen. The app handles the math — you focus
on the game.

🧮 Built-in Primiera calculator
Stop arguing about whose hand is better. Tap in the cards each player
holds and the calculator tells you the winner instantly.

👥 Reusable player profiles
Create players once with a name, color, and emoji. Pick them at the
start of every game — no more retyping. Stats follow them across every
game they play.

📊 Real statistics
Win rates, average scores, head-to-head records, win streaks, and
per-category breakdowns. See who is actually the Settebello king.

📜 Searchable history
Every completed game saved automatically. Filter by player, sort by
date or score, see exactly when Marco beat Giulia for the fifth time
in a row.

⚡ Quick start
Recent player groupings appear on the home screen — one tap to start
a game with the same lineup as last time. Pin your favorites for
instant access.

🇮🇹 Italian and English
Full localization for both languages, because the game deserves both.

What we don't do:

✅ No ads — ever.
✅ No accounts, no sign-ups, no email required.
✅ No tracking, no analytics, no data leaves your phone.
✅ Works completely offline.

Free. Open-source. Yours to use, share, and build on.
