# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-05-28

Scopa Score Tracker is made by **Vinciguerra Games** to track scores for
the Italian card game Scopa. We believe that what you do in the app is
your business, and so the app is designed to collect as little
information as possible.

An Italian-language version of this policy is available at
[`PRIVACY.it.md`](./PRIVACY.it.md).

## What we collect

**Nothing.** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal
information.

## Where your data lives

Everything you create in the app — player profiles, in-progress games,
completed games, statistics, language preference — is stored locally on
your device. None of it leaves your device.

## Third parties

The app does not use:

- Advertising or advertising identifiers
- Analytics or telemetry
- Crash reporting that includes user data
- Social media trackers
- Third-party servers of any kind

The app works completely offline.

## Your data is yours

Because everything is local, you can:

- **Export your data** at any time (when the export feature is
  available — see [issue #45](https://github.com/taterhead247/scopa-score-keeper/issues/45))
- **Delete everything** by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage
- **Move devices** by exporting from one and importing on another

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect any information about anyone, including
children. It is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that collect or transmit data (for example,
optional anonymous usage analytics to help us improve the app), we
will:

1. Update this policy with clear details before the change ships
2. Always require your explicit opt-in
3. Never collect personally identifiable information

You will always know what is being collected and have a way to turn
it off.
